Basic Commands:

  • "Open [App]" - YouTube, Gmail, WhatsApp, etc.
  • "What's the time?" - Get current time
  • "Weather" - Check weather
  • "Tell me a joke" - Get a random joke
  • "Calculate [math]" - Simple math calculations
  • "Stop" - Stop listening

Advanced Commands:

  • "Call [Contact]" - Make a call
  • "Message [Contact] [Text]" - Send SMS
  • "Turn on/off [Device]" - WiFi, Bluetooth, Flashlight
  • "Battery status" - Check battery level
  • "Navigate to [location]" - Open Maps
  • "Play music" - Open Music player
  • "Show me news" - Open News
  • "Who is [person]?" - Search information

Tips & Tricks:

  • Speak clearly for better recognition
  • Use exact app names when possible
  • Commands are case-insensitive
  • Check microphone permissions in browser
  • Works best with stable internet connection
  • Some features require HTTPS or localhost
  • Try both English and Hindi commands
  • Voice output requires speaker/headphones
